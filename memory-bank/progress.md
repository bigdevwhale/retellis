### Email verification for local-account signup (2026-08-20)

Flag off → identical to today (`email_verified=true`, endpoints 404, no email). - 2026-07-24 (Joint family session — fourth fix: the ACTUAL cross-member visibility root cause, reported 3×): the bug was **client-side, not server-side**.
